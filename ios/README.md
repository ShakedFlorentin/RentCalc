# הטיול ליפן: iPhone app

Native SwiftUI app for the same trip and the same Supabase project as the web app, plus home-screen and Lock Screen widgets.

## Run it on your iPhone (on the Mac)

1. Install Xcode from the App Store, open it once, and install the iOS platform it offers.
2. `brew install xcodegen`
3. In this folder: `make open`. This creates `Config/Supabase.xcconfig` from `../.env`, generates `JapanTrip.xcodeproj` and opens it.
4. In Xcode, pick the **JapanTrip** target, then **Signing & Capabilities**, and choose your Apple ID as Team. Do the same for **JapanTripWidgets**.
   If Xcode says the App Group is taken, change `APP_GROUP` in `Config/App.xcconfig` and the bundle ids in `project.yml` to something of your own.
5. Plug in the iPhone, choose it at the top of Xcode, and press Run.
   The first time, on the phone: Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, then trust your developer certificate.
   On iOS 16 and later also turn on Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode.

With a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days; press Run again to renew it. A paid developer account removes the
limit and allows TestFlight for the rest of the group.

## What's in it

| | |
|---|---|
| `App/` | the app: sign-in, Home (countdown or day X, today's plan, Japan's clock, the route), Plan, Bookings, Places, More |
| `Widgets/` | **Countdown** (small, and Lock Screen circle, line and inline) and **Today's plan** (medium, large, Lock Screen) |
| `Shared/` | used by both: the rows (`Models.swift`), Japan's calendar (`TripCalendar.swift`), the widgets' copy of the trip (`WidgetSnapshot.swift`) |
| `project.yml` | the Xcode project, generated by XcodeGen; the `.xcodeproj` itself is never committed |

The widgets don't sign in: after every load the app writes the whole trip (a few KB) into the shared App Group, and the
widgets read it. They keep working offline and turn over to the next day at Japan's midnight on their own.

Still in the web app for now (More links to it): Discover (the swipe cards), Expenses, Lists, Essentials, the translator,
adding places and bookings, sign-up with an invite. Live sync between phones comes next; until then the app reloads when
it comes to the front and on pull-to-refresh.
